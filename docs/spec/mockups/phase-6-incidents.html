5. Placing a camera that has no clock

Chapter 1. Match by sound, the file's own time, or by hand. How it was placed becomes a pill the camera carries everywhere.

Place a camera

BWC2-0451 has no clock in its picture

Its place on the incident clock is not known. Three ways to set it, best first.

Match by sound

The app compares this camera’s sound with a placed camera’s and finds where they line up. Works when the two were within earshot; about a minute on the worker, nothing goes to the engine.

Matched lines up 41.3 s after BWC2-098679 starts; the match is strong.

The file’s own time

The camera wrote 21:56:58 into the file when it started recording. Camera clocks are often a little off, and nobody checked this one.

Placed this way the camera is marked From its file, unchecked.

Place by hand

Drag its bar on the strip until the same moment lines up on both, then nudge.

+41.3 s

Play both with the sound from one and listen for the echo to vanish.
