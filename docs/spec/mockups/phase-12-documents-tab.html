Phase 12, a new chapter proposed · three directions for the maintainer's word · 2026-11-06

The Documents tab as the case's landing place for its documents and comparisons

The ask of 2026-11-06: the Documents tab holds the reports used for comparisons inside incidents, and the pictures saved from the Map land there too; it should be the place where a person sees the saved pictures, adds the documents, and reads a report against the whole case or against an incident, so the document features leave the incident page, and a comparison is a sub-page under the tab. Below: what the tab and the incident page hold today and where it hurts, three directions for the tab, the comparison page and the add flow they share, the incident page afterwards, and the choices. The fictional office throughout: the case Harbour Street stop, its incidents Harbour car park and Walk incident, 6 Nov, the cameras Lee Fox, Kit Vale, Ash Kim, Jo Marsh.

Where the features are, today. On the incident page: a Report tab in the work panel (the linked reports' pages), Add the report on its Details tab, the Comparison layer over the Report tab, and Comparison to Word in Export. On the recording page: the same Report tab and Add the report. On the Documents tab: the list, Re-link, and the comparison against the case as a layer under the table. On the Map page and the incident's map pane: Save a picture, which needs an incident or a camera to keep the picture on.

Where it hurts. 1 The lead sends a person elsewhere to add a document. 2 A document must belong to an incident or a camera: the home decides which comparison is possible, and a picture must be kept "on" something. 3 One column reads the comparison against the case; the incident's comparison of the same report is on another page and not shown here. 4 Re-link is two drop-downs on every row. 5 A picture sits in a table built for reports, with a column that says it has no words. 6 The findings open as a layer under the table: on a long list the layer is below the fold, and the report's page is a card press away.

A document belongs to the case. The home goes: a report is the case's, with an optional about (an incident or a camera) that no longer decides anything but what Gideon reads it beside and which record is offered first when you compare. The two Re-link drop-downs fold into More ▾ (About…, Rename, Remove).

Every comparison of a report, one row each. The case, an incident, a camera: as many as the person asked for, side by side under the report, each with its findings as a four-colour bar (agree, differ, not on camera, not in the report), its counts, when it was read, a stale pill, and Open to its page. Compare against ▾ names the record: the case, each incident of the case, each camera with a transcript.

The pictures are a gallery, the picture itself the thumbnail, with Open, Download and On the map (the Map page at that day and moment). A picture may be about an incident or a camera, or about nothing: Save a picture no longer needs something to keep the picture on.

Add a document is the page's one primary, and a dropped PDF adds without a page. The chips narrow the shelf; a stale comparison is one chip away.

One grid, two kinds. A report's card shows its first page as a cover; a picture's card is the picture. The comparisons are rows inside the card as in A, so nothing the shelf says is lost.

What it costs. With a dozen reports the bars and counts are no longer in one column to scan down, and a card's width caps what a comparison row can say; the shelf's table reads faster once the case has more than a few. The look's rule (a row-per-thing table becomes cards under 700 px) already gives this shape on a narrow window, so B is what A turns into there.

The address. case/<id>/document/<doc>/comparison?against=case (or against=incident:<id>, against=recording:<id>); the case bar's Documents tab lit with "› Comparison" after it, as the Map page sits under its tab. Against ▾ in the title switches to this report's other comparisons, or starts a new one. The old ?tab=documents&compare= address lands here.

What is drawn is what the layers draw today (the marks, the filter pills, the rows with Make it a note, Note, Dismiss, Choose the moment on a Not on camera finding, By recording for the case), with the report's page beside it so the paragraph is read in place instead of in a card; By page is new, the findings in the report's order. The moment plays in the Preview window (v1.166.0), never leaving the page.

Against an incident the rows carry the incident's clock and the camera as today, and the moment plays every camera in the Preview's all-cameras shape; against a camera the recording alone. The reading itself (the windows, the second look, the child rows for the case) does not change.

What fits: PDFs only · 60 pages at most · 12 documents a case (this case has 3) · a scan is read by OCR and its words may carry mistakes · nothing leaves the building.

Two radios become one optional about. Today the page asks "this report is about: the whole incident / one camera's own report" and must have an answer. Now a document is the case's; the about is a tag, and the one it had (the incident or camera it was linked to) is what every existing document gets on the upgrade.

Compare when it is read saves the second visit for the common case (the incident report, read against the case); it is off unless pressed, so an upload never starts a nine-minute reading by surprise.

The settings. "Documents per incident or recording" (3) becomes Documents per case (12 as shipped, the Admin's figure); Largest document, OCR, the reading ceiling and the comparison's figures stay. A dropped PDF on the shelf adds with the file's name and no about.

The chronology's rows keep every note made from a report: "Note by D. Rowe, Lee Fox, from the report, page 4, paragraph 2", resting on the paragraph, the Paragraph card opening from it as today.

What leaves the incident page: the Report tab and its pages (the document page and the comparison page show them), Add the report on Details, the Comparison layer, Comparison to Word in Export. What stays: the note events from a report with their origin and the Paragraph card, the memo's reading of a rested-on paragraph, and Gideon's reading of the reports about this incident or compared with it.

Details gains one line: the reports compared with this incident with their counts and a stale pill, Open the comparison, the documents about this incident, and Compare a report with this incident ›, which lands on the Documents tab with the record chosen (the shelf's menu open, or C's form filled). A person who starts from the incident still gets there in one press.

The recording page the same: its Report tab and Add the report leave, its Details gets the same line, its comparison runs from the shelf against that camera. Both pages lose their one upload page for documents; add-document?incident= keeps working as the about pre-chosen.

The build's recommendation is Direction A with the comparison page, and the incident and recording pages giving up their document features (5 and 6). It answers the ask directly: one landing place for the documents and the pictures, a report read against the case or an incident at the person's choice, the comparison a page of its own under the tab, and the incident page one tab lighter. The data model carries most of it already (a Comparison has the case, the incident and the recording as homes; a Document has its incident and recording, which become the about), so the release is pages and words more than a migration. Direction C's ledger stays drawn for a day when a case has many reports; B is what A becomes on a narrow window.

Two things to say plainly. Phase 8 chapter 4 ruled out "a document for the whole case with no incident or recording" to keep the reading small; Phase 10 chapter 4 then read a report against the whole case, so the rule was already half gone, and this chapter ends it. And the glossary's Document entry, the Report tab entry and the Comparison entry would be amended, with one new word, the Comparison page; "about" needs an entry too if it stands.
