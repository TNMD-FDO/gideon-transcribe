Drawn 2026-10-21 for the maintainer to pick, after reading the test_2 call back: 202 proposals from the words on 573 lines, six checks and four reprocesses, and the call fixed in the end by naming voices, one Sounds like settling 167 proposals at a press. The page today is a list of proposals with the voices underneath; this drawing turns it over. Every name and line is made up.

The order. The voice does its work as the transcript lands: the cast as voices with samples, the hand-overs, the Sounds like against the case's prints, and the words' name hints as evidence. Stage 1 names the voices. Stage 2 is only the lines the voice puts in doubt, each with its three sounds. Stage 3 runs the words once against the named cast and shows what little is left. The engine's work moves later and shrinks; the voice's work moves first and carries the names.

What stays. The line-up with lanes, the cards and their samples, the picker, Sounds like, the hand-over, the re-read after a name, Run again, Undo, the strip's pill. What changes is the order the run does its work in and the shape of the page.

The words run too, but only to find names, and what they find is shown on the card as evidence with the quote and Play, never as Accept or Reject. Name or merge... opens with the hinted name typed, so taking it is one press and ignoring it costs nothing.

Nothing about lines is proposed yet. The transcript reads plain under the cards. On test_2 this is the difference between 202 proposals and none, at the moment the person first opens the page.

The order of the cards is the order of the work. The most talking first; the Call system last, with no controls. A Sounds like is the quickest way through a card, and the biggest: on test_2 one Accept named 387 lines.

A named voice folds. One line: the name, how it was named (by voice, by hand, split at a time), its count, one sample and "change", which opens the full card again. The unnamed cards stay open and move up, so the eye goes to what is left.

Each name re-reads the rest (v1.144.1): the prints learn, a new Sounds like can appear on another card, the lines in doubt are re-scored, and the stage 2 count on the strip moves. Nothing waits for Run again.

Going on early is allowed. A voice nobody can name stays a label; the next stage's lines still work on it. The bar says how far the cast is named, not whether the person may continue.

The voice decides what is in doubt. A line is here because the voice puts it near another named speaker, cannot tell, or hears two voices in it. The words are read against the named cast and appear as the reason, agreeing or not; a words-only proposal with the voice silent is folded, never on the page by default.

The words' reading arrives after the voice (added 2026-10-21 at the maintainer's question). Stage 2 opens on the voice's list at once; the model is then asked one narrow question about just these lines, with the names, roles and Also called names in hand, and its reading lands on the rows a few seconds later, as the page already polls. With the engine unavailable every pill reads "the voice alone" and nothing else changes. The model is never asked about labels: both of its readings, here and at stage 3, come after the names.

The one primary is the agreement. "Accept the 11 the voice and the words agree on" is the one-press path; the voice alone waits for a press; the voice against is red and never in the one-press path, as today.

Mixed lines are split here, at the word, with both parts to play, instead of being moved whole or fixed by hand line by line, which is what the 7 single-line moves on test_2 were.

Counts, not a flood. On a call like test_2 this stage is tens of lines, because the voice is asked first and the words are asked about the named cast.

What this is not

Kept as it is

Nothing is named by the voice alone. Every name is a person's press on a card; every move is a press on a line. The one-press path is the agreement of voice and words, as today.

The picker, the samples, Sounds like, the hand-over, the re-read, Undo and the lanes are unchanged in what they do; they move on the page and gain the stage they belong to.

The phone keeps its one column: the stages strip at the top, the cards in a row, then the lines of the stage in hand.
