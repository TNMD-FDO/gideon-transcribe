The maintainer's ask after v1.173.0: the bulk page offers two options where the Upload page's "2. Settings (optional)" offers the lot. What the build does, and the one thing for your word
  1. One settings card in the app, not two. The card is cut out of the Upload page into a piece both pages draw, and the script that reads and writes it is shared, so a setting added to one page later is on the other. The Upload page does not change for a person using it.
  2. Per-file exceptions are drawn in (figure C). Say if the bulk page should carry batch settings only, with each file's title and nothing else per file; the fold and the line would then be simpler, and a person who needs exceptions would use Upload files.
  3. No model, no migration: the batch's settings reach the app through the same submit as the Upload page's.
