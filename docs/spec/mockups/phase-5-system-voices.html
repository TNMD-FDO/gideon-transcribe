The phone system's voice, kept for the office; and a deleted case's chain

Two things the maintainer asked for on 2026-10-26, and the guards they asked for the same day against a person taken for the system. The jail phone system's recorded announcements are already given to the Call system by their words; the second half of that plan teaches the app the system's voice from those lines, so on every jail call the lines the words missed are the system's too. The voice is the office's, not a unit's, and sits on the Voices place under its own tab. And a kept voice whose case has since been deleted says so. The names are the fictional office's.

What the tab holds
  • One card per phone system the office meets. The first is made the first time a jail call lands with enough Call system lines (three or more by their words) once the switch is on. A later call whose system lines do not sound like any known system makes a second card, "Call system 2", which an Admin may rename ("County jail phone system"). Most offices will have one.
  • Taught, never named. The print is the mean of the system's lines on every call that taught it, weighted by seconds, exactly as a unit's book is taught. Nobody keeps it from a card and it is never a person; nothing of a party's voice goes into it, since only lines already the Call system's teach it.
  • Hear it plays a few seconds of an announcement in the right pane, as a unit voice's Hear them does.
  • Off at first (the maintainer's worry of 2026-10-26). A new phone system starts listening, not moving: the voice step counts the lines it would have given and the card says so ("would have given 41 lines on 6 calls this month"), and nothing on a transcript changes until an Admin presses Start giving lines by its voice, having judged the numbers on the office's own calls. Stop giving lines by its voice goes back to counting. Forget deletes the print; it is made again from the next call, off again.
  • Called out. Three lines moved back by hand in a month stop the giving by themselves and the card says so, as a unit voice turned down three times is called out; an Admin starts it again when they are satisfied.
  • Who sees it. Everyone who has the Voices place; the tab is the office's. Renaming, stopping and forgetting are an Admin's.

How a line is given by its voice
  • On a jail call the voice step already reads every line's own voice (the diarizer container, since v1.139.0). In that same reading, before the check and the name suggestions run, a line not yet the Call system's may be given to it by its voice. The worry is a person taken for the system, so five guards stand in the way, all constants judged on the office's calls, none a setting:
  • Taught from the words only. The print is made from lines the phrase list found, never from a line given by voice, so a party's voice never teaches it.
  • Nearer the system than its own speaker, by a firm margin. The line must be closer to the phone system's print than to every party's print on that call, by the margin the check uses for a sure move. A person who sounds like the system is still closer to their own print on nearly every line.
  • Next to the words. Only within a short gap of a line the words found (announcements come in runs: the opening, the one-minute warning, the close), or when the match is very strong. A lone mid-call line is never taken on voice alone.
  • A ceiling per call. If the voice would take more than a small share of one party's lines, it takes none on that call and the page says "held back": that party sounds like the system, and a person should look.
  • Counted and reversible. Every line given by voice carries the pill and Not the system; three moved back in a month stop the giving (figure 1).
  • Every line given by voice carries the pill and Not the system, which gives the line back to the label the recogniser had, undoable as a rename is, and counts against the print on the Voices place ("moved back by hand"). The lines given by words have no way back, as now.
  • The memo, the exports and the check see the Call system exactly as they do today; nothing new reaches the engine.

The setting. No new row: the Call system switch covers the voice half, and the voice step runs where Voices help the check lists Jail call. With the Call system off, nothing here runs and the tab is absent.

Stored. One row per phone system in a table of its own (the vector, the name, the counts and dates, the parts per call); a migration. Never logged, exported or sent anywhere, as the books are.

Audit. Counts and who only: phone system first heard, renamed, stopped, forgotten; a line moved back by hand. The lines given by voice go in the transcript's provenance as a count, as the words' lines do.
