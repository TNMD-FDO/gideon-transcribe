As built in v1.197.0, kept. Within a circle draws one at the map's centre; dragging anywhere inside moves it and the answer follows; the handle widens it; Make an incident from the circle sits under the answer and opens the New incident box with the inside cameras ticked, the day's others under them, and the span from the first entry to the last exit. Nothing is made without the press.

The only circle. This is the circle, the one the person drew. The map draws no other, and "Make an incident from the circle" appears only while a hand-drawn circle is on the map.

Three faults. The "same day" suggestion, every video of the day, draws as one chip across the whole row, which says nothing. The three path suggestions sit on one spot of a six-hour day, so their chips are the minimum width and pile into rows with their words cut to "4 cameras, 1…". How it works. Each suggestion is a numbered pill at the start of its span, with a thin line in the incidents' colour under it for the span's length. Pills that would land on one another step to the right, in order, so every number is readable; the row is always one row high. The words are on the card below (Figure D) and on hover; a press lights the card and the card's hover lights the pill. The same-day suggestion gets no pill at all.

Where they land. On the Incidents tab, where they are today: directly under the strip, above the Not on the clock panel, three or four to a row and wrapping on a narrow screen. Nothing of theirs is on the Map tab. A press on a pill scrolls to its card and lights it; a hover on a card lights its pill.

Numbered to match. Cards 1 to 3 are the pills 1 to 3; the same-day card comes last, unnumbered, with no pill, and only when the day holds videos in no incident. "Make an incident" opens the New incident box filled, on this tab; "Not this" puts the suggestion away for the case.

On the map opens the Map tab on that day with the suggestion's cameras ticked and lit and the map fitted to them. Whether it also draws the circle round them is choice 10 below: the person pressed for it, so it is not the map offering a circle unasked, but the maintainer's word on the circle is "the user draws it", so the drawing recommends no circle here either.
