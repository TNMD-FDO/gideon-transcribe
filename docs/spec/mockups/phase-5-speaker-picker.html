The speaker picker: whose is this line?

Drawn 2026-10-20 for the maintainer to pick (the voice plan's section 13). Today "not this speaker" opens a box that asks for a typed number, and the recording page answers only to the number keys. One control instead, wherever the app asks whose a line or a voice is, and never a typed answer unless the person is new. Every name and line is made up.

The same list, in the same order, everywhere: On this call, In this case, Someone new. Picking a person from the case puts them on the call as a speaker; Someone new makes the person in the case and the speaker on the call in one step. Number keys, arrows and Enter work as before.

Added 2026-10-20, after the maintainer's question "what if the sample is wrong?": where the voice step has run, a speaker's samples are the lines nearest their voice print, never a length-chosen line that may be mislabelled; the play button cycles the three with a count; a person's sample from another recording opens in the floating Preview with that recording's title.

On this call lists the speakers as the cards have them, in the cards' order, with their colours, number keys and a play button for the speaker's sample, so a voice can be heard before it is chosen. The one the line has now is struck through and cannot be picked; the Call system is never offered a person's line.

The sample is the voice's, not a guess. Where the voice step has run, each speaker's three samples are the lines nearest their voice print, one from each third of the recording; a mislabelled line sits far from the print and is never one. The same choice feeds the "hear voice 1, hear voice 2" buttons on every proposal. Where the voice step has not run, the length rule stays, with stuttered lines kept out (v1.141.0). The play button cycles the three, 1/3, 2/3, 3/3: three lines that disagree with each other are the sign of a mixed label, which the Speaker check is for.

In this case, not yet on this call lists the Speakers tab's people who have no lines here, with role, where they appear and their Also called names. Their sample is in another recording, so Play opens the floating Preview (figure 5) with that recording's title, as the case chat's citations do. Picking one gives the line to that person and the person appears as a speaker on this call, with a card and a lane, at once.

Someone new opens the name box the cards already have (figure 2). Outside a case the second group is absent and Someone new makes a plain new speaker, "Speaker 4".

No typing to choose. The keyboard still works: the number keys as today, now including the case's people; arrows move the light; Enter picks; Esc closes. The sheet closes when the line moves.
