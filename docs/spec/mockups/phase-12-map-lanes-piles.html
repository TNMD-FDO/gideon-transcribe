Notes that overlap on a lane

The ask (2026-11-10, after v1.227.0): on the Map page's lanes, when notes overlap you cannot see the one under it. It reproduces on the local demo, and it is worse than it looks: the note underneath cannot be pressed either.

What happens today (figure 1)
  1. Each note is drawn at its own moment as a tag 8 px wide. On a lane that spans hours, 8 px is several minutes, so notes made within a few minutes of each other land on the same pixels and the last one drawn covers the rest.
  2. Measured on the demo at 1366 wide: three lanes of four notes each, and on every one of them three or four of the notes sit inside one tag's width; on two lanes some sit less than 2 px apart. A wider screen (2560) spreads them a little, but the piles stay.
  3. A press reaches only the tag on top, so the notes under it can be reached only by stepping with the card's arrows, if you know they are there.

Three ways to show them follow. The words are made up; nothing of a real case is on this page.

P1. A pile with its countNotes that would overlap are drawn as one wider tag with a second leaf behind it and the number on it, standing at the first note's moment. A press opens a short list of them in order of time; a row opens that note. A note alone stays the plain tag. Works for two notes or twenty, and nothing is moved off its moment.
P2. Fanned apartOverlapping tags are pushed apart so each shows and each can be pressed; a small bracket under them says they were moved off their own moments. Reads at a glance for two or three, but the tags no longer stand at their own moments, a pile of ten takes 100 px of the lane and runs into the next mark, and every lane grows a few pixels taller for the bracket.
P3. ShingledEvery other tag in a pile is dropped and the one before it raised, so a corner of each peeks out, all at their own moments. No extra height and no list, but past two notes it is a smudge again (the orange lane holds four), and the corners are too small to press with any confidence.

Figure 4. P1 at real size, light and dark

A pile is 15 px wide with its second leaf, against 8 px for a note alone, so the two read apart without the number; the number is the label size the ruler uses. Notes join a pile when they would stand within a pile's width of its first note, so a busy stretch reads as a row of piles ("3", "4", "2"), never one long block, and the piles re-form by themselves when the lanes show a shorter or longer stretch.

My pick: P1, the pile with its count. It is the only one of the three that still works when a lane holds many notes in a few minutes, it keeps every mark at its true moment, and it reuses the list and the "2 of 4" stepping the map already has for pips on one spot, so it is a small build with nothing new to learn.

What it would change: the Map page's lanes only (and the same lanes wherever the map script draws them); the map's own pips, the Notes tab and the incident page's strip stay as they are. The specification's Phase 12 strip line gains an amendment, as v1.227.0's did.

Two riders, yours to take or leave: (a) the list could also name the other marks under a pile (a lights or siren mark hidden by the tag); (b) a number above 9 widens the pile to 19 px, as the "12" in figure 4 shows; the alternative is "9+".
