The Note window: one way to play a note from the Notes tab, in Rows and in Chronology

Today a note's time does two different things on the same tab. In Rows it leaves the case page for the recording page. In Chronology it opens the Preview, which since v1.165.0 has no column to sit in and so slides up as a sheet from the foot. The drawing gives both one door: a clean floating window, the frame the hearing window already has, showing the right recording at the note's moment with the note under its line. Nothing new is invented: it is the Preview card, as it already plays citations and search hits, in the window shape the office has used for voices since v1.154.0.

The time is a play mark now. ▶ 20:26:10 opens the window; the row lights while its window is open, as a cited hit does today. The list stays where it was, so the next note is one press away and the window simply moves to it.

The window is the hearing window's frame. The accent head with the recording's title and the moment, Close in words, drag by the head, its place kept for the next one on this page, Escape closes. The office already knows it from the Speakers page.

What it shows. The picture at the moment with the camera's own stamp, played from a few seconds before; the lines around the moment with the note's line lit; the note itself under its line with the writer and the date. Everything the Preview already draws, only the note block is new in this shape.

The foot. Note writes or changes the note in place (the Preview's own box); Open the recording goes to the recording page at the line; All cameras where the recording is a synced camera of an incident. "on the page" and "all cameras" stay on the row for the eye that goes there.

Choices, answered 2026-10-28

Two things only the maintainer could decide

  1. What the note's words do in Rows. Answered: only the time opens the window; the words keep leaving for the recording page with the box ready. Today they leave for the recording page with the box ready. Recommended: the words open the window too, one door for the whole row, and Note in the window's foot edits in place; Open the recording is one press further. Or: the words keep leaving the page and only the time opens the window, which keeps Phase 8 chapter 2 as written but leaves the row with two behaviours.
  2. The page behind the window. Answered: no dim. The hearing window dims the page lightly while it is open, which suits a judgement on a voice. Recommended for notes: no dim, since a person plays several notes in a row and reads the list beside the window as it plays. Or: the same light dim as the hearing window, for one look everywhere.

What does not change. The Notes page (its own page, the player on the left) plays a row in its own player as now. The Search tab's note hits already open the Preview, so they take the window with no work. A note on an incident's event plays the camera it sits on at that moment, with Open the incident in the foot, as the chronology's event entries do.

The spec. Phase 8 chapter 2's row citation ("opens the viewer at that line") and Phase 10 chapter 1's "in the page's own right pane" both become the Note window, with the sheet under 900 px. An amendment line in each; no new word in the glossary (Preview and Note are the words).

Size. One release, no model change, no migration: the rows' two attributes in the template, the window shape in preview.js and app.css (the hearing window's frame without the voice parts), and the tests for the door and the shape.
