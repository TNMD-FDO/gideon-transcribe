The unit's voice book: officers' voices kept beyond one case, checked by hand, per unit

Drawn 2026-10-21 for the maintainer to pick before the voice plan's release V4b is built (ADR 0021 amended; Phase 5 chapter 2 gains a section). Every name, unit, call and line here is made up; the office's own unit names and directory paths are values the Admin types, never words in the app.

What changes and what does not. V4 keeps a voice print inside one Case and matches it there on its own. This release adds one more place a print may live: the unit's voice book, where a person who may open the case chooses to keep a Person's voice, and only a Person in a role the Admin allows (officers, interpreters; never a client, a defendant, a witness or a victim). The book never proposes a name by itself: a card is checked against it by hand, on the recording's Speakers page, the one place a proposal is accepted or declined. Units come from the directory: the Admin lists each unit with the directory folder its accounts sit under, and every sign-in reads the account's folder. A person in no unit sees none of this. An Admin sees every unit's book, side by side, and acts in a case as the case's unit does.

Four things on four pages. A new place on the rail, Voices, listing the unit's book (figures 1 and 2). A line under a Person on the Case's Speakers tab, Keep for the unit (figure 3). A button on an unnamed card, Check against the unit's voices, and what it answers (figure 4). The Admin's units, how an account is placed in one from its directory folder, the roles allowed and the switch (figure 5).

One place, named for the unit. Its line reads the unit's name as the Admin typed it, so a person in the Appeals unit sees "The Appeals unit's known voices" and a person in the Trial unit their own. The two never see each other's book, and the book has no page for the other unit at all.

When it is absent. The place is not on the rail when the Admin's switch is off, when the person's account sits in no listed unit, or while Folder management or Remember voices across a case is off. Nothing in a case shows then either: no Keep for the unit, no Check button.

An Admin sees every unit. Their rail line reads "Every unit's known voices" and the place opens with a chooser, one tab per unit (figure 2). An Admin's own unit is still read from the directory, or set by hand for a Local admin (figure 5); it decides where their own cases keep voices, not what they may see.

What this page never shows
No line of any recording, no time, no name of a case the reader may not open (it reads "a case you may not open" and counts). The voice is heard only through Preview, from a recording the reader may open; where there is none, the Preview is greyed with the reason.

One table, one row per voice. Name with Also called, Role, the Voice (how many cases and recordings taught it, Preview, the chain), who kept it and when, and what the checks against it decided. The same Voice column the Case tab has, so the two read alike.

Forget is the row's one action, danger, with a box like Unlearn's: the book's print is deleted; names accepted from it stay (people accepted them, on their cards); every case's own print stays. The row stays a while, greyed, with "Keep again from a case".

The dismissal line is V4's, carried over: three Not-them in a month, in words, with the way out.

Who may open it. Everyone in the unit, to read, hear through Preview and Forget; every Admin, for every unit, the tabs above the table picking the book. Forget is an act of the unit, written to the audit as counts and who. There is no Accept here: a check is made and decided on a recording's card (figure 4).

The box
Keep Ana Villar's voice for the Appeals unit?
Her voice print is copied into the unit's voice book, where anyone in the Appeals unit can check an unnamed speaker against it by hand, on any recording in their cases.
  • Nothing is named by it on its own. A match is proposed only when someone presses Check, and is accepted or declined on that card.
  • This case keeps teaching the book while she is kept: each name or match accepted for her here refines the book's print. Other cases that accept her from the book teach it too.
  • Her role, Interpreter, goes with the voice. Her notes, Also heard as and this case's name do not. The book says "kept from" this case only to people who may open it.
  • Stop keeping here, or Forget on the Voices page, deletes the book's print. This case's own print stays either way.
Written to this case's audit and the unit's: kept, by whom, when. No voice and no line.
CancelKeep for the Appeals unit

Opt-in, per Person, by a person on the case. The line appears only under a Person with a print, in an allowed role, in a case whose owner sits in a unit with the switch on. A role outside the list reads one muted sentence and offers nothing. Changing a kept Person's role to one not allowed stops the keeping, and the box says so.

The case stays the teacher. The book's print is the case prints' average, weighted by how much each heard; Unlearn in a case takes that case's share out of the book too. So the book is never more sure than the cases behind it.

Which unit. The case's owner's unit, read at their sign-in. An Admin on the case acts in that unit, whatever their own. A collaborator from the other unit sees the Person kept "for a unit you are not in" and no actions; that is the one time a unit's name is not shown.

Name or merge...Check again

Pressed, never automatic. The case's own prints keep proposing Sounds like on their own, as V4 has it; the book answers only when asked. The button sits beside Name or merge on every unnamed card, with one line saying what it compares and that the answer lands here. It is greyed with the reason when the voice did not listen on this recording (its type is not under Voices help the check) or the speaker has too little clean voice.

The answer is V4's Sounds like block with one more pill, "the unit's voices", and a Preview from a recording the reader may open, or "heard on a case you may not open" and the samples alone. High and medium only; a book print taught by one case rates medium at best. The conflict offers both, as in a case.

Accept does three things and says so: adds the Person to the case with the book's name and role (or names the Speaker as an existing Person of that name), names the lines, teaches. Undo reverses all three. Not him is written to the book's count and to nothing else. No match is a plain line with Check again.

Which book. The case's owner's unit's, for an Admin too: the one book a case has. A named card has no Check. The Side rule's alarm and the case's own matches stay as drawn in V4.

Sign-in settings
Units
The office's units, one per line: the unit's name, a vertical bar, then the directory folder (OU) its accounts sit under. At each sign-in the account's own folder path is read and the first line it falls under is the person's unit. An account under none is in no unit. Changing a line takes effect at the person's next sign-in.
Appeals | OU=Appeals,OU=Staff Trial | OU=Trial,OU=Staff
How an account is placed, read at every sign-in
The rule
  1. The directory names every account by a path: the account's own name first, then the folders it sits in, nearest first, then the domain. The sign-in already reads this path to prove the password.
  2. The path is cut at its commas. The account's own name (CN) and the domain parts (DC) are set aside. What is left is the folder chain.
  3. A Units line matches when its folders appear in the chain in the same order and next to each other, letter case ignored. A line with one folder matches any account anywhere under a folder of that name.
  4. The first matching line wins, so a unit nested inside another is listed above it. No match: the account is in no unit.
  5. The unit's name and the chain it was read from are kept on the account, as words. An account moved between folders changes unit at its next sign-in, and the nightly directory check reads the folder again and says what moved.

Units are a list, not a model. One text setting; the unit's name and the folder chain are kept on the account at sign-in as words, next to the directory address the account already carries, and the People page shows both so a wrong placement is seen, not guessed. No office value is in the app: the folder paths are typed by the Admin and the drawing's are made up.

The roles allowed are a list the Admin owns, drawn from Speaker roles, with the three roles that name a client's side or a witness refused outright, so no setting can put a defendant's voice in a book.

Off is honest and loud, as V4's switch is: the counts per unit before the throw, the books gone at once, the cases untouched.

A Local admin's unit is picked by hand on the People page, since the account is not in the directory. A directory account's unit cannot be changed by hand; the list and the folder decide.

What this is not

Left as it is, and what is kept

Nothing is named by the book alone. No automatic match from the book, no Accept all, no list of "voices found" anywhere. A press, an answer on the card, a person's Accept or Not.

Nothing crosses a unit. A book is read and written by its unit only; a case owned in one unit keeps to that unit's book; a collaborator from the other unit sees that a voice is kept and nothing more. An Admin reads every book and gains nothing more in a case: the check there uses the case's unit's book.

Nothing of a client's side is kept. The roles allowed exclude Defendant, Victim and Witness by rule, and a Person with no role is not allowed either.

What is stored. One vector per kept Person per unit, with name, role, who kept it and when, and which cases teach it, in a table of its own; the unit's name as a word on each account; the checks' outcomes as counts. Never logged, never exported, never sent anywhere, deleted by Forget, Stop keeping, Delete person in the last teaching case, or the switch.

The ADR. ADR 0021's rule "nothing crosses a Case" is amended to "nothing crosses a Case on its own: a print may be kept for the unit by choice, for allowed roles, and matched only by hand", with this drawing as the record.

Phones. The Voices table stacks as the Case tab's does under 600; the card's Check button wraps under Name or merge.
