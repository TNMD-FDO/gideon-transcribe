An incident from a span, without making incidents twice

The chronology already holds what an incident needs: the recordings on one clock, their spans, the notes at their times. Chapter 2 makes one from a span with one press. The worry is the second press: a note written later, a hint seen again, and a second incident over the same hour. The drawing adds three guards. The case's incidents are drawn on the chronology, Make on a span that overlaps one leads with Add to it, and a later note on a camera outside the incident says so and offers to add the camera. Nothing is ever made without a press. Made-up case.

The incidents lane. One bracket per incident of the case, under the Notes lane, in its own colour with its name, camera count and event count on it. Hover says the cameras and the span; a press opens the incident. A person cannot make twice what they can see. Chapter 3's deferral of incident events on the chronology stands; this is only their spans.

Two doors to Make. With a span dragged, the lead offers Make an incident from this span (chapter 2 as written). A hint's bracket carries Make an incident for its own span, so the common case is one press with no drag. Both open the picker (figure B).

Nothing is made by itself. No proposal, no incident from a rule. The hint is a signal; the person decides.

As chapter 2 has it. The recordings with bars in the span come ticked, up to the eight most noted; the rest wait behind Choose more. The count reads against the Panel's Most cameras in an incident, and in hours; the sitting bar redraws with each tick. A camera with no notes is tickable, and a note on it afterwards becomes an event.

The name is offered from the day and the span's start, and can be typed over. The incident's About gets one line: "From the case's chronology, 6 Nov 20:24 to 20:40".

One incident per span by default. When the span touches an incident, the sheet names it and leads with Add: the cameras not yet in it come ticked, the ones already in it are shown and cannot be ticked twice. Make a new one anyway stands second, in the quiet style, so a second incident over the same hour is a choice and never an accident.

Widening. When the span runs past the incident, Add widens the incident's span to cover both and says the new span before the press. The cameras already in it keep their places on its clock. (Choice 2 below.)

Two incidents touched. The sheet names both and leads with the one that holds more of the span; the other is a line with its own Add. Rare, and nothing is merged by rule.

A note on a camera already in the incident is an event of it the moment it is written (Phase 8 chapter 11). Its entry says so with the incident's pill. Nothing to do.

A note on a camera outside the incident, inside its span, gets one quiet line under its entry: inside which incident, that its camera is not in it, and Add. The camera arrives on the incident's clock as it sits on the chronology; the note is then an event like the others. The line is the only invitation the page makes, and it never says "make an incident".

A spell inside an incident carries the incident's pill on its heading, so the eye sees the hour is already covered before it reaches the hint.

Choices, answered 2026-10-28

Two things only the maintainer could decide

  1. Where the incidents lane sits. Answered: under the Notes lane. Recommended: under the Notes lane and above the hints, so the marks, the brackets and the hints read top to bottom as "what was noted, what already exists, what might be made". Or: above the ruler, beside the rail, as a strip of the day's incidents.
  2. What Add does when the span runs past the incident. Answered: widen, saying the new span first. Recommended: widen the incident to cover both and say the new span before the press, one audit row. Or: add the cameras only and leave the incident's span as it was, so the notes past its end stay outside it until someone widens it on the incident page.

What does not change. The incident page, its own sync by sound and by hand, Propose events and the sitting bar are as they are. Incident events never appear on the case's chronology (chapter 3). No incident is made or merged without a press.

Size. One release, no model change; one migration if the About's "From" line is kept on the incident (a span and a source), else none. The spec: chapter 2 amended with the lane, the Add-first sheet and the entry's line; Appendix A gains "Incident made from the chronology" and "Incident widened from the chronology".
