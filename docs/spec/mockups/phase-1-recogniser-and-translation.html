Nothing is added to a line. The same lines, times, speakers, notes and clips; only the words change. The switch is remembered in the browser as the open tab is. A line said in English carries a small "en" on the As spoken side, since it has no other text.

A Correction is made on the words as spoken (the switch moves to As spoken when Edit is pressed) and that window's English is written again, marked "English to follow" on the line meanwhile. A Find hit in the other text opens with that text showing and says "in the words as spoken".
