The rule: one copy, saved once

A case has one Owner, and the Owner may mark people it is shared with "Also an owner". For the Preservation copy, all of them are its owners (your decision). The risk is that two of them each save it to the client's file. Four things together prevent that.

1Everyone is told who else has itThe message names every person it went to and says "only one of you needs to save it".
2The file is not handed to several peopleWhen the copy goes to more than one person, the message carries a link, not the file. Whoever downloads it is recorded the moment they do.
3One mark, seen by allThe first download marks the copy "Downloaded by M Jones, 18 Nov" for everyone. The reminders stop for everyone that night.
4The others are told it is doneEach other owner gets one last line, "saved by M Jones, nothing more to do", and Download asks before giving the file a second time.
What has to change in what is on the server: today a case's sharing list goes with the case, and only the one Owner sees its copy in Archived. From this release the app records who was marked Also an owner when the case goes, and shows them the copy too. Delete, Restore and Remove now stay the Owner's and the Admins'.

Why the attachment is Off by default and On for you: the app is also installed by other offices, and an attachment sends privileged work through the office's mail system. You have said yours may; another office should choose. Your mail's 30 MB limit holds a zip of about 21 MB once encoded, so 20 is the right number for you.

The picks

One answer to each, please; my recommendation is marked.

PickThe choicesWhy I recommend it
1. Several owners and the file1a A link, never the file, when the copy goes to more than one person recommended
1b The file attached for the Owner alone; the others get the link
1c The file attached for everyone		If two people each have the file in their mailbox, nothing in the app can tell either that the other saved it. With a link, the first download is recorded and everyone sees it. Most cases have one owner and still get the file.
2. Someone who saves it from the email2a The message has a "press here so you are not reminded" link, and the row a Mark as saved button recommended
2b Nothing: the reminders carry on until they download it from the app or its days run out		You asked for reminders until it is downloaded. A person who saved the attachment has done the job and should be able to say so in one press.
3. How often the reminder3a Every night until it is downloaded or its days run out, as you said recommended
3b The first night, then weekly, then nightly in its last week		It is what you asked for and matches how the app already warns about cases. It can mean up to 30 messages for someone who ignores it; 3b is there if that is too many.
4. A case the clock deletes4a One message the night it goes to the Recycle bin, then the nightly reminder; no second message when it is wiped recommended
4b A message only when it is wiped and its copy reaches Archived		The night it is binned is when the owner can still restore the case, so that is when they most need to hear.
5. Download again5a Once someone has downloaded it, Download asks first, Figure C recommended
5b Download never asks; the row's "downloaded by" is the only sign		It is the last guard against a double save, at the moment it would happen.
Said so it is not a surprise: the message never quotes anything from the case; it names the case, the people and the dates, as the app's other messages do, and the file when it is attached. This release changes the written rule that the app's mail carries no attachment (the dictation message is the one exception today), so it comes with its chapter and an addition to the decision record. A copy made from the Details tab at any time sends no mail: that one you asked for yourself.
