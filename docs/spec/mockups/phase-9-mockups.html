Mark reviewed, and what Home says

The recording page’s head in three states: before, with the “Assigned to you” pill and Mark reviewed as a plain button; the one question, in both its forms; and after, with the green pill, the Undo toast and Not reviewed after all as a quiet ghost button. The fourth panel is the Notes page’s foot in call mode. Then Home’s rows: “6 of 80 left for you” is a plain pill, never in the warning tone, so a case with your calls waiting is not under Needs you.

2. One question before the mark

Mark call 0471 as reviewed?

You have left 2 notes on it. The team will see it as reviewed by you, today.

You have left no note on it. Mark it reviewed with nothing to note?

The question carries what the app knows, so a call with no note is a conscious answer and not a slip.

Open the call: a pop-out, never a jump away

Open the call, from the left’s head or a group head’s Open, does not leave the Notes page. The whole call opens in a pop-out over it, the chat’s Preview grown up: the full transcript with Follow, the player with Boost and the sides, the waveform with the notes marked, the note editable under its line, the summary and the speakers beside, Mark reviewed and Export in its head. Esc or Close returns you to the notes exactly where you were; Open the full call is the one way out to the recording page, for its clips, its Speakers page and its chat.
