The Incidents tab as a strip: incidents forming from the notes, and videos placed by sound

Three things the app already has are brought onto one strip at the top of the tab: the case's videos on their clocks (the chronology's band), the brackets that appear wherever videos overlap under notes (the chronology's rule, drawn dashed here because nothing is made yet), and the sound matcher that finds how far one recording's sound runs after another's (Match by sound, which today only serves the incident page). The strip is read from the same data the Notes tab's Chronology shape reads, so it changes as notes are written and as videos are placed. Below it, at most three cards, then the incidents as they are now. The fictional office of the local demo throughout.

The table as it is today: each video, the clock read from its picture, how it was placed, and the incident it is in. Folded while every video is placed or offered; open when one is neither.

The strip is the chronology's band without the spells. The same days, ruler, bars and note marks the Notes tab's Chronology shape draws, read from the same code (one day in view, the busiest day first, the others a press away). The notes lane keeps its marks alone; the words stay on the Notes tab. The strip takes one height whatever the day holds, by the band's own three shapes (stacked, packed lanes, the Coverage lane over six).

Two lanes under the marks. The incidents lane as chapter 2 drew it, one solid bracket per incident. A forming lane under it: a dashed bracket wherever two or more videos overlap on the clock with notes under them (the chronology's own rule, today's brackets with Make an incident). A bracket with no notes under it is drawn faint and makes no card, unless every video in it carries a seen clock, which is today's offer and stays one.

At most three cards. Each forming bracket with a card is one card under the strip: its span, its cameras, where the notes gather, the sitting bar, and Make it (the chapter 2 picker with those ticked), Choose myself, Not these. Not these folds the bracket to faint for good, as declining an offer does today. More than three: the three with the most notes, and "and 2 more on the strip".

Sound places a video, it does not make an incident. A video with no clock in its picture sits on the rail, as on the chronology. Match by sound, which today serves the incident page, runs for it on the media worker against the placed videos of its day (its file time first, then the day): a clear match (a spike, not a plateau) is one line on the rail with Place it there. Placing it writes the recording's clock as by sound, with the match's strength and the video it followed, and the bars and brackets redraw: the video now overlaps, and may join a forming one or an incident's offer. One mechanism for all of it.

It moves as the case moves. A note written on the Notes tab, a video landing, a clock read: each redraws the strip on the next draw, and the tab polls by the minute while open as the chronology does. The card count in the lead ("1 forming") is the only number that changes above the strip.

What stays. New incident, the incidents table, the videos table (folded), the Panel's settings: Incidents proposed governs the cards and the brackets, Match by sound the rail's line, Most cameras the picker. The incident page, the chronology's own brackets and the picker are unchanged; this tab reads them.

For your word

What it would take, and what to decide

Recommended: Figure A, as two releases. First the strip with the forming lane and the cards, read from the chronology's day shape and the chapter 2 picker, no new store beyond the declined keys the offers already keep. Then placing by sound: the media worker's match of a clockless video against its day's placed videos, a new way of placing in the recording's clock (by sound, with the match's strength and the anchor), one migration, the Panel's Match by sound widened to cover it.

  • Words. "Forming" for a dashed bracket and its card (the glossary gains it; a forming one is never called an incident, a proposal or a suggestion). "Placed by sound" joins clock, by hand, from its file and by its day in the Recording's clock entry.
  • Rules to confirm. A forming bracket needs two or more videos overlapping and at least one note under the overlap; without notes it is faint and silent, except today's seen-clock offer, which keeps its card. Three cards at most, the most noted first. Not these folds a bracket to faint for good. The sound line appears only for a clear match (the spike rule the matcher already has), and never places a video by itself.
  • Cost. The strip is one more read of the day shape the Notes tab already builds, cached per draw. A sound match is a few seconds on the media worker per pair and runs when a clockless video lands or a video is placed, against the placed videos of its day only; no engine call, nothing leaves the building.
  • Settings. No new setting: Incidents proposed governs the brackets and cards, Match by sound the rail's line, Most cameras the picker.
