The Notes page as a shape of the Notes tab

Today the Notes page is a page of its own, reached by a button in the case's head and another on the Notes tab, with panels of its own for the Recordings, the Chronology and the Details, which are now tabs and shapes of the case page. The drawing makes it the Notes tab's third shape, Rows | Chronology | Page, drawn inside the tab like the other two; the button beside Add recordings opens the tab in that shape; and the page's own doorways and duplicated panels go. What stays. Everything the Notes page does: the notes as the wide left column in call order with the pills by writer and type and To check, the Find box over the notes and the recordings' words, Previous and Next note (or call), the recording in hand on the right with its one player bar, the waveform with the notes marked, the lines around the moment with the note under its line, Note at this moment, Mark reviewed, Open the call, Listen through the recordings. The keys are the page's as before.

What goes. The page's own panels for Recordings, Chronology and Details, which are the Recordings tab, the Chronology shape and the Details tab now and were duplicates; its Export menu, whose two downloads sit on the lead as Download notes and, in the Rows shape, on the Details tab. The page's own head and its Back to the case went with the case bar already.

Where it draws. Inside the tab's content, the full width the tab has (the case page is already the wide layout), the two columns side by side from 1100 pixels and stacked below it, the recording in hand first when stacked (figure C). The pressed note on the chronology plays here too when this shape is the one open.

The old address keeps working.
/case/<id>/notes-page goes to /case/<id>?tab=notes&shape=page, so a bookmark or a link in a guide still lands on the notes. The page's own template and its list and find addresses are kept for the shape's script, which draws the same thing in the tab; nothing is rebuilt.
The glossary.
Notes page keeps its name and becomes "the Notes tab's third shape"; the toggle's word is Page. Nothing else changes its name.

Stacked below 1100 pixels the recording in hand comes first, so the player is at the top where the thumb is, and the notes scroll under it; the Notes page already stacks this way on a phone. From 1100 the columns sit side by side.

Size. One release, no migration: the shape in the toggle and the address, the page's script mounted into the tab's content with its panels and Export menu dropped, the head's button pointing at the shape, the tab's own Notes page button removed, the old address redirecting; Phase 9 chapter 4 amended to say the page is the tab's third shape.

Nothing a person does on the page is lost. Thirteen things stay as they are, one is reached a different way (the two downloads on the lead), and three go because the case page already has them as a tab or a shape: the Chronology panel, the Details panel and the head's three buttons. The script that draws the page is mounted into the tab unchanged except for those three.

Listen through the recordings is the one I nearly dropped: it is the page's own flow, the recordings as cards with Listen and Mark reviewed, stepping call by call, and not the Recordings tab's list. It stays, as the left column's second list.

Choices

Two things only the maintainer can decide

  1. The shape's word on the toggle. Recommended: Page, short beside Rows and Chronology, and the glossary's Notes page is "the Page shape of the Notes tab". Or: Work, saying what the shape is for.
  2. Where the head's Notes page button lands. Recommended: the Notes tab in the Page shape, which is what the button has always opened. Or: the Notes tab in whichever shape the person last used, with the Page shape only from the toggle.

What does not change. The Rows and Chronology shapes, the Preview's window from a note's time in the Rows shape, the Notes tab's pills, Search notes and Download notes, the recording page's notes, the exports.
