The lanes' look on the Map page

The ask (2026-11-10): on the Map page's chronology the marked notes blend in too much, and the timer disappears a bit. Both reproduce on the local demo, and both have a plain cause.

What is wrong today (figure 1)
  1. The timer. The clock on the playhead is an 11 px label at the very top of the lanes, drawn under the ruler row: the ruler is a sticky strip with its own background and sits above the playhead, so it covers the label's lower part, and the lanes' own head ("5 of 5 shown") covers it from above when the band scrolls. What is left is a sliver. It also reads to the minute while the map's clock runs to the second.
  2. The notes. A note is a mark 3 px wide and 14 px tall in the accent blue, on top of a lane bar that is 10 px tall in the camera's colour. On a blue camera the two blues differ by a shade (measured: the mark #0f5e86 on the bar #1f6fb2), so the note is invisible; on any other colour it is a thin line the same height as the lights' mark.

My pick: T1 for the timer and N2 for the notes. T1 fixes the real fault (the clock drawn under the ruler) without spending height the lanes band does not have on a laptop, and makes the clock larger and to the second, as the map's own clock is. N2 gives a note the shape the incident page's strip already taught, in the page's ink so it never depends on the lane's colour, three times as wide as today's mark and the lane's full height. T2 is the alternative if the flag over the ruler's labels bothers you; N3 if notes should never touch a bar.

The same marks elsewhere, if wanted: the map's own pips for notes (on the paths) are the same accent blue on a blue path; they could take the ink-and-pale-edge treatment in the same release so a note looks alike on the lane and on the map.
