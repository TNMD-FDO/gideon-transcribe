Figure 3

Inside a colleague's chat

Opening a colleague's chat says whose it is in one line at the top, and each question carries the name of the person who asked it. Carrying a chat on is allowed, as today; your question goes in under your name, so the thread reads as a conversation between the team and Gideon rather than one voice.

In your own chats, nothing changes: no notice, and the stamps stay the time alone.

What does not change

  • Every chat belongs to the case, is open to whoever can open the case, and leaves with it. The Phase 2 principle "nothing is private inside a shared case" stays, with one sentence added: the drawer shows yours first.
  • Export to Word, Delete chat, the audit rows and the recording page's and incident page's chats are untouched.
  • An Admin who is not a member sees Everyone's as today, with their access audited as today.
  • Someone who is the only person on a case sees no tabs at all: the list is theirs already.
