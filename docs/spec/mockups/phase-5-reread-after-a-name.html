The re-read after a name: what the page says while the proposals catch up

Drawn 2026-10-21 for the maintainer to pick, after their find on v1.144.0: once a speaker is named, the check's proposals no longer take the change into account. The fix chosen is the local re-read ("A"): after every rename, merge, split or Sounds like accepted, the app rewrites each pending proposal with the current names, closes what the name settled, re-matches the unnamed voices against the case's prints and re-scores the voice, with no engine call. This drawing is how the page tells the person that is happening, and what changed. Every name and line is made up.

Three rules. It never blocks: the name lands at once and the re-read follows in the same second. It says what it did in counts, on the page, in the voice the check already uses. And it is honest about its limit: the lines are re-read against the new names; the words are not, and Run again is how the words get re-read.

The toast is the one moving part. It appears as the name lands, with the small spinner the app uses for a job in hand, and goes when the re-read is done, usually within a second. The page never waits for it: the cards, the lanes and the counts change at once as they do today.

The head's line is the record. One line under the check's state, in the accent tint the check uses for a note, said in counts: settled, re-read, closed, newly sounds like. It stays until the person dismisses it or the next run, so somebody coming back to the page sees why the proposals read differently from an hour ago.

The limit is in the same sentence. "The words were read before the name; Run again re-reads them too." No second warning box: the fact sits where the person is already reading, with the button beside it.

The reason is rewritten with the current names and the voice's word re-scored; a block that the re-read touched carries one quiet pill; a proposal the name settled stays in place, tinted, with what settled it and Undo, as an accepted one does today.

Nothing jumps. The rows stay where they are, in reading order. A settled row keeps its place and its tint so the reading never loses a line; Undo on it is the viewer's Undo of the name, which puts the proposal back as it was.

One pill, not a banner per row. "re-read for Dana Reyes" sits on the block head of each run of lines the re-read touched, and on a single line's row when it stands alone. It goes with the next run or when the head's line is dismissed.

The voice's word may change. A line the voice could not tell before may now agree or disagree, because the prints were rebuilt from the cast as it stands. The pill's tint moves with it; nothing is accepted by itself.

A closed suggestion says why, once. The name the words suggested for the label is struck through with "closed: this speaker is Dana Reyes now" and a dismiss; the row goes with the next run. Nothing silently vanishes from a card the person was looking at.

A match born of the re-read says so. The print taught a second ago can match another label on the same call, which means two labels may be one person. The pill says the match is new and why; the button says plainly that Accept is a merge on this call; the Preview plays Dana from this very call, since that is where her print came from.

Nothing else on the card moves. Samples, not this voice, Name or merge and the hand-over keep their places; the re-read adds a line or strikes one, and that is all.

Quiet when there is nothing to say. The same line, without the tint and without counts, so the person knows the re-read ran and the page is current.

The strip's pill carries the fact to the recording page. "re-read for Dana Reyes" after the count, until the next run; a press opens the Speakers page as today.

Where the words stay stale. After the re-read the check's state line keeps its time ("Checked today 11:52"), since the words were read then. Only Run again moves it. The head's line is what says the lines were re-read since.

What this is not

Kept as it is

No engine call. The re-read rewrites, closes, re-matches and re-scores from what the run kept; Run again stays a person's press (an automatic Run again is a later Admin choice if wanted).

Nothing is accepted by itself. A proposal the name made true is marked settled with Undo, as the merge already does today; a new Sounds like waits for a press.

Where it lands. Phase 5 chapter 3, "The Speakers page", one amendment: the re-read after a name and its line; the Sounds like block gains the "new" pill. One release, v1.144.1.
