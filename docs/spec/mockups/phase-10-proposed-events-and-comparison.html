Phase 10, a new chapter · a drawing for the maintainer's word · 2026-10-29

Proposed events and the comparison, read over the whole case

Today the assistant proposes events and compares a report only inside an incident. This drawing gives both a place on the case: a Proposed events tab in the chronology's shape, and the comparison on the Documents tab read against every recording of the case. One rule stands under both: a proposal is a moment on a recording, it touches nothing until a person accepts it, and accepting writes a note on that recording. A note already follows its recording everywhere, onto the case's chronology and onto any incident the recording is synced into, so the accepted item appears wherever it should with nothing else built. The order you gave. Recordings, Search, Proposed events, Notes, Documents, Incidents, Clips, Speakers, Details. Proposed events hides as the button does today: when the assistant is Off and no watch phrases are listed. Its count is the proposals waiting.

The chronology's shape, with a proposed lane. The same days, ruler and band as the Notes tab's Chronology, the recordings on the case's clock, so a proposal is placed by the clock its recording already has. Above the notes lane a lane of small triangles for the proposals, the assistant's in the warning tone and the watch phrases' in the incident colour. A recording with no day yet puts its proposals under Not yet placed, in order of time into the recording, as the chronology does with its unplaced notes.

Propose events reads every recording in the case that has a transcript (answer 2), the watch-phrase search over all of them first, the engine's windows after, with the time expectation under the button. Running again replaces the proposals still waiting and never proposes again what was dismissed, as the incident's run has it. A recording that landed since the last run shows as "not yet read".

Accept is one press (answer 1): the sentence becomes a note on that recording at that line, or at the moment when nothing was said, in the acceptor's name. Accept and edit opens the note box with the sentence in it first. The accepted entry stays in the list, greyed, saying what it became and whose; its why line stays readable here.

A proposal on a camera of an incident says so with the incident's pill, and the spell inside an incident's span wears it on its heading, as the Notes tab does. Accepting it writes the note, and the note is an event of that incident at once (Phase 8 chapter 11).

Where it sits. The Documents tab gains one column, Against the case, and one button per document: Compare with the case, or Comparison when one exists. The layer opens over the table in the shape every layer has, with Documents as its Back. The comparison inside an incident and on a recording are untouched.

How it reads. The document is read against each recording of the case in turn, one run per recording, the way the recording's comparison already reads a transcript and its Digest, because one record of the whole case does not fit a call. The findings gather on one layer, each naming its recording, ordered by day and time on the case's clock, with By recording as the other order. The state line says the count of recordings and the time it took; the time expectation shows while it runs.

Make it a note replaces Make it an event: the claim becomes a note on that recording at the moment, in the person's name, with "from the report, page 4, paragraph 2" kept on the note's link back. A Not on camera finding has no moment, so Choose the moment opens the Preview's window on a recording the person picks, and Make it a note sits in its foot. A finding already made a note says so and dims.

Stale. As today: a comparison goes stale when a recording's transcript is made again or a recording joins or leaves the case; the state line says so and Compare again refreshes.

Nothing new is carried. The note is a note (Phase 8 chapter 2) and follows its recording wherever the recording is used: the Notes tab, the case's chronology, every incident the recording is synced into, now or later (answer 3). That is the rule the app already keeps.

One small thing kept on the note: which proposal or finding it came from, so the source pill, the why line and the AI notice survive the change from an Event to a note. The inside of an incident reads them through that link and loses nothing it prints today.

The other way round is already true: a note written on the recording page, in the Preview's window or on the Notes page is an event of any incident the recording is synced into, and sits on the case's chronology, the moment it is saved.

Choices, answered 2026-10-29

The four answers, recorded

  1. Accept. Answered: one press writes the note; Accept and edit beside it.
  2. What the whole-case runs read. Answered: every recording in the case. No picker; the time expectation says how long.
  3. The incident's own Accept. Answered: it becomes a note too, and a note added anywhere follows its recording everywhere, including an incident it is in. The second half is already the app's rule since Phase 8 chapter 11.
  4. The proposals waiting on incidents today. Answered: stay as they are until accepted or dismissed.

What the spec changes. Phase 7 chapter 2 and Phase 6 chapter 3: an accepted proposal is a note, not an Event of its own source; the source and the why live on the note's link. Phase 8 chapter 2, principle 1: a note may be an assistant's sentence a person adopted by accepting it, and says so. Phase 8 chapter 4: the comparison may read a document against the case, one recording at a time; "a document for the whole case" stays ruled out, since the document keeps its home. Phase 10 chapter 1: the chronology stays notes only; the proposals have a tab of their own. Each amendment is written into the chapter it changes, and the glossary gains Proposed events tab and amends Event, Note and Comparison.

What does not change. The proposer's judgement, the watch phrases, Look for, the four marks, the memo, the incident page's layers in look, the Notes tab, the case's chronology. No proposal or finding is ever shown on a recording page until accepted.

Size. Three releases, each walked locally first. One: a Proposal table on the recording with the link from a note, the incident's layer and comparison writing notes, nothing else visible changing; one migration. Two: the Proposed events tab, the case-wide run, the Preview's window's strip and foot, the tab order. Three: the comparison against the case on the Documents tab and its export.
