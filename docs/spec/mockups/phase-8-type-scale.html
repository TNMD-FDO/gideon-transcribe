The sizes off the scale, beside the scale

The look allows five sizes, 22, 17, 15, 13 and 11, and a display size for a figure. The stylesheet still sets 12 pixels in 35 places and 14 in 14, with a few 16, 18 and 20. This drawing wears the app's own stylesheet, so each pair below is the component as it is today beside the same at the nearest size on the scale. Made-up office.

The guided Speakers page's question (16) to the heading size, its second line and a person card's name (14) to the body size, the "4 of 9" figure (20) to the title size. Six rules in all. The recommendation is to move the first three and leave the figure at 20, written into the look as the size for a count beside its words, since 22 is a page title and 28 a clock.

For your decision
  1. The tables: 15 (recommended), 13, or leave at 14.
  2. The rail and the top bar's name: 15 (recommended), or leave at 14.
  3. The dense parts: 13 (recommended), or leave at 12.
  4. The figures: as the fourth pair, with the count left at 20 and written into the look.

Whatever the pick, it is one release, stylesheet only, with the look's Type section amended to say what it says and a test that no size off the scale remains.
