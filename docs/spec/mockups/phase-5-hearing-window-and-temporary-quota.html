The hearing window, and a quota raised for a while

Two things the maintainer asked for on 2026-10-23. The first changes where the Preview of a person's voice sits on the guided Speakers page: a window that floats over the page and is plainly its own thing, with a Close anyone can read. The second gives an Admin a way to raise one person's space for a set time, after which it comes back down on its own, without pulling the floor out from under what is already stored.

Plainly a window. The card floats over the page at the bottom right, with a shadow, a coloured title bar in the app's accent that names what is being heard ("Hearing Tony Carruthers") and a Close button in full words at the bar's end, white on the bar so it cannot be missed. Escape closes it too. The page behind dims a shade while the window is up and the row whose button opened it keeps a ring, so the eye knows which voice this is for.

Not the page's column. Nothing on the guided page moves or hides: the question stays where it was, the context lines stay in their column. The window can be dragged by its bar and comes back where it was left. Under 900 pixels it is a sheet from the foot of the page as now.

The same snippet inside. What the window carries is what v1.152.0 settled: the person, their role, the clock pill, a tab per recording that taught the print, the samples as plays, the line heard with one line either side, a small player that plays the line and stops, Open the recording. Nothing is logged.

Everywhere a voice is heard. The case page's Speakers tab and the Voices place open the same window; the chat's citation and the search's hit keep the in-page panel they have, since there the page is the reading and the player belongs beside it.

Raise for a while...
RaisedThe person's space is the raise's figure until its date. The storage warning near the limit and every refusal name the raised figure. The user sees one quiet line on Home while it runs: "Your space is 100 GB until 6 December, then 50 GB again."
Coming back downThe days ran out while more is stored than the usual figure allows. The quota becomes "what is stored now, and no more": nothing new fits, nothing is deleted, and as cases go under the Retention policy or the person deletes, the figure follows the storage down until it reaches the usual quota, when the raise closes by itself. The person reads why on the Upload page.
ClosedBack at the usual figure, by the date or by the storage coming down to it, or ended early by an Admin. The row keeps the last raise as one grey line for the record.
RecordedAudit rows: "temporary quota raised" (figure, days, note), "temporary quota ran out", "temporary quota closed", "temporary quota ended early"; no name in any detail beyond the affected account. One setting, "Temporary quota length", 45 days as shipped, is the form's default; the Admin may type another.

Why "no more than is stored", not a cut. A quota that drops below what is already kept would have the app refuse the person everything and tell them to delete, at once, on a date they did not choose. Holding the line at what is stored keeps the promise the Admin made while the raise ran and lets the storage come down the way it always does: the Retention policy's 30 days without activity, or the person's own deletions.

One figure to reason about. A person's space is one number at any moment: the usual quota (the default or the Admin's standing override), the raise's figure while it runs, or what is stored while it comes back down. Every place that reads the quota today, the upload check, the warning, the users list, the Status page, reads that one number from one function.
