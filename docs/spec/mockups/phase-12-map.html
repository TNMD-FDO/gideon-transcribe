Phase 12, a new chapter · a drawing for the maintainer's word · 2026-11-02

The Map tab: every camera's path from its own file, on one clock, with the video a press away

The ask of 2026-11-02: some body-worn and in-car video comes with a separate file beside it holding the camera's position second by second (and for a vehicle its clock, speed, lights, siren and brakes). A tab in the case should take those files in and show the cameras moving on a map: zoom and pan to see which are in view, scrub a clock and watch everyone move, press a camera and its video plays in step; the readings (lights on, siren, the microphone muted) should be smart data the chat can answer from. Answered the same day: the street map comes in the first release from one file the Admin places on the server (nothing reaches outside the office); the word is Path, made of Fixes, from a Path file; a camera's clock comes from its own file first, then the clock the app already holds, then the minute in its file name; files come in through this tab, matched to their videos by name. The fictional office of the local demo throughout; no real place is drawn.

Readings. Everything a file says beyond where: lights and siren on and off, brakes, the crash sensor, a bookmark press, the microphone on and muted (with how long), low battery, and two things worked out from the fixes: stood still (no movement for over two minutes) and the top speed reached. Each is a time of day; Open moves the clock there and opens the camera; Offer to the chronology makes a proposed event the person accepts into the case's record, beside the notes.

Who was near here. A press on the map asks which cameras came within a chosen distance of that spot and when, sorted by arrival. The answer is from the fixes alone.

The chat. The Ask Gideon drawer is on every page in a case already. From the first release the case chat reads the readings with the transcripts: one short paragraph per camera in the office's time of day (when it began and ended, lights, siren, mic, stood still, who was together) and a rule to say which camera and cite the moment. A cited moment opens the Preview as citations do today; on the Map tab it also moves the clock. Positions themselves are never put to the engine, only times, distances and the readings' words.

Three ways to draw the movement, one control, remembered per person: Markers only (heads move over the faint whole paths, nothing grows behind them), Trails (the solid part grows behind each head as the clock runs, as Figure A), Whole paths (every path solid all the time, the heads riding on them). A fourth press hides the faint whole paths altogether under Markers only.

Events. The readings that are moments, drawn where they happened on the map and when on the lanes: a camera turning on (a ring at the start of its path, a hollow pip on the lane) and off (a square at the end, a filled pip), lights on and off, siren on and off, the mic on and muted, a bookmark press. The Events ticks choose which kinds show, on the map and the strip alike.

Pressing one. On the map or the lane, a press selects the event: the clock moves to its moment, the camera is lit, and a card says what it is, where (the street from the map file when there is one) and until when, with Play here, Offer to the chronology (the third release), and the previous and next event of that camera. The transport's ‹ event and event › step through every shown event of the day in order. Escape clears the selection.
