Where. A last tab, Details, with no count, after Documents. The word is the one the Notes page and the incident page already use for the same kind of panel; the glossary needs nothing new.

What it holds. Everything the column held, as blocks in two columns: the case's facts and its actions; the Team as it is, with Also an owner, Remove, Leave and Transfer; Names and places as it is; the downloads gathered in one block, which the case list and the notes downloads join from their tabs' heads (they stay there too). Delete case alone at the foot, after everything, as before.

What changes elsewhere. Nothing in behaviour. Rename, Share, Transfer, Delete and the Team's buttons keep their script; the page simply draws them on one tab. The head's pills, the One reading strip and the tab bar stay on every tab.

The Preview. It opened at the top of the right column for a citation or a hit. Without the column it floats as it does on pages with no right column, over the page's corner, and the Chronology's play uses the same.

On a narrow window the two columns of blocks stack into one, facts first.
