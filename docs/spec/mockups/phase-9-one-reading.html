A case is read differently: whole transcripts packed into Readings of about six hours of talk each, under a ceiling of hours, and over the ceiling the overviews first. This drawing gives the case the same honesty in one strip on the case page and two lines in the drawer, from figures the app already keeps. Made-up cases and figures.

Red: over the ceiling. The bar is the ceiling; the hatched part is what is over it. The pill in the head appears only in this state and opens here.

Where it sits. Under the head's pills, above the tabs, on every tab of the case page, as the sitting's bar sits on an incident's Cameras tab. It is drawn from stored figures and reads no transcript to draw.

The three states. Green under one Reading; amber under the ceiling but more than one; red over the ceiling. The thin mark at 85 percent of the ceiling is the nearly-full line, the same as the sitting's.

What the figures are. Hours are the recordings' lengths, as the ceiling counts them (Case chat hours, 120 as shipped). Readings are whole transcripts packed in upload order into about six hours of talk each, from each transcript's size kept on its row. Summarised is a live count.

When a file is added. It counts under "not ready yet" until it lands, as a question would skip it. When it lands, its size is kept with it and the strip moves the first time the page is drawn after. A correction, a translation landing or a summary written refreshes that one transcript's figure.

Room for. "Room for about N hours more" is the ceiling less the hours. In amber it is still said, because the ceiling is the thing that changes how a question is answered; the next Reading is only minutes.

Nothing new to set. The ceiling, the Reading size and the Readings at once are settings the app already has. The strip shows only when the Case Chat is on, as the sitting's bar shows only with the assistant on.

The head line. Today it says "This case". It gains the strip's figure in a few words, so the person sees what a question will cost before typing. With a narrowing on the page it says the narrowed count, as today.

The grounding line. The sentence the drawer already shows, with the Readings count added in the amber state. The over-the-ceiling sentence is the one that exists; it changes only "hours of talk" to "hours of recordings", which is what the figure is.

What it read. Under every answer, the drawer already keeps what the question read. It gains the Readings count and the time taken, so a slow answer explains itself and a fast one shows what it skipped.

Not drawn. No new setting, no new page, no change to how a question is answered. The strip and the lines describe the rule of Phase 9 chapter 6 as it stands; they do not change it.
