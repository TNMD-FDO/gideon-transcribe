The question comes from the hand-over. "Split here..." opens the picker beside the card asking From 4:12 the voice is, with the count of lines after that time and the first of them. Every line of Speaker 2 from 4:12 on goes to whoever is picked; the lines before stay.

Three kinds of answer. An on-call speaker or a person of the case takes the lines (Dana Reyes, Riley Park). A new speaker on this call is lit by default, as today: the next free label, to be named later from its card. Someone new names them now: name and role in one step, the person joins the case and the call together.

The name box takes the keys. The number keys and arrows choose a row until you type in the name box; from then Enter is Name, Esc clears the box first and closes on a second press. "Someone new" with a role is the only place a typed answer is ever needed.

After. The new speaker's card appears under the one it came from, lit, with a one-time pill saying where it came from, its own three samples by the voice print, and the Undo line at the top of the page reads "Undo: split Speaker 2 at 4:12". Speaker 2 keeps its first lines and loses the hand-over box.

The rule follows the column, not the window. Today the controls drop under the words only when the whole window is under 900 px, so on a full-width screen the transcript column can be 780 px and still squeeze. After, the transcript measures its own width: under about 1000 px the controls take their own line under the words, with the words at full width; wider than that they stay on the right as drawn. Built at 1300 (v1.143.0): the transcript's reading width is capped at 1040 px, so beside the controls the words would have about 440 px at most; the line was drawn where the words really gain from it, and on every common screen the controls sit under the words.

The controls do not change, only where they sit: the voice pill, the run number, Play before and after, Accept and Leave, in the same order. The block head's Accept all stays where it is.

What this is not

Kept as it is

The page shape. The line-up with lanes, the three columns over 1500 and the band and transcript under it, the player and the lanes on the right. Nothing moves columns.

The keys and the sounds. Number keys, arrows, Enter and Esc in the picker; the page holds still while it is open; one sound at a time.

The check's strip and the Speaker check panel. Their proposals, Accept all per run and Accept per line are unchanged; only the row's layout is.

Where it lands in the plan. Phase 5, chapter 3, as an amendment to "The speaker picker" and "The Speakers page": no new chapter, no new words. One release, v1.143.0.
