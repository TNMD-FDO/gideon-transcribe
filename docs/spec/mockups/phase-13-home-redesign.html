Phase 13, a first chapter proposed · three directions for the maintainer's word · 2026-11-06

Home redesigned: less to read, nothing that needs you, and what the team is doing, or not

The ask of 2026-11-06: Home is confusing and should carry no Needs you information; how, if at all, could it carry information relevant to collaboration. Below: what Home holds today and where it hurts, what the app actually knows about colleagues (the only honest basis for a collaboration line), three directions drawn as whole pages, and the choices. The fictional office throughout: the cases Harbour Street stop, Pike calls, Marsh Lane interview; the colleagues Pat Okonkwo and Sam Reyes; the person looking is test.

Where it hurts. 1 Every section opens with a sentence to read before anything is used. 2 Needs you puts a case first because one incident has one pill, and the heading is amber before the person has done anything. 3 The incidents of every case are listed under its row with sync pills, so one case fills the screen with six lines of state that belong on the case's own page. 4 A row carries five kinds of pill in two tones, and the one that is about the person ("4 of 4 left for you") is lost among the case's.

What the app knows about colleagues, which is all a collaboration line can be drawn from: who a case is shared with and when each last opened it; who is assigned which recordings, who reviewed which and when; who wrote each note and when; who asked Gideon what, on a case; a dictation sent to you. There is no message, no mention and no presence in the app, and nothing is proposed here that would need one.

Your work replaces Needs you. The one thing on Home that is about the person: the recordings assigned to them and not reviewed, by case, with Next (the oldest, as the recording page's Next offers it) and the way to all of them (the Recordings tab, Mine, not reviewed). Drawn only when something is assigned; a person with no assignments sees no section.

Cases are rows, not dashboards. The name, what it holds in one muted line (recordings, incidents as a count, whom it is shared with by first name), when it was last used and by whom, Open. No state pills: a case's state is on its page's Dashboard line, where the person is about to act on it. The incidents' lines under each row go.

What leaves. Needs you and the amber heading; every pill on a case row; the incident lines; the sentence under each section's heading (the rail's line already says what Home is for; the keep-or-lose line moves to small type at the foot of This session). The rail's line for Home becomes "Your work, and your cases".

What the team did, from what the app already records. Five kinds of line and no others: reviewed (reviewed_by and reviewed_on), noted (the note's writer and time, folded per person per case per day: "left 2 notes"), shared with you (the Share's added_on), added recordings (who moved or uploaded them into a shared case), asked Gideon (a case chat turn by a colleague, with its question, which the drawer already shows to everyone). A dictation sent to you joins them. Each line opens where the thing is. Twenty at most, newest first; Seen folds the section to its heading until something new happens, remembered per person.

What it is not. Not a feed of the person's own work (they know), not a mail, not a count of anything, not a place to write. It draws only from cases the person is on, so it shows nothing an Admin's audited look would not. An office of one person never sees the section.

Why it earns its place. The Team block on a case's Details tab says who has what; nobody opens five cases to learn that Pat finished the calls. This is the one screen where "what happened while I was away" can be answered, and it is the only collaboration information the app can state truthfully.

A card per case: the team as initials, the counts, a bar of how far the review has come, the person's own left, who used it last, and Next. The collaboration is in the shape (whose case, who is on it, how far along) rather than in a list of events.

What it costs. A person with twenty cases scrolls a wall of cards, and Ready to download, which the chapter put first for a reason, drops below them. The bar means nothing on a case where nothing is assigned, which is most of them today. The initials are a new drawing with no name on them until hovered.

The build's recommendation is Direction B: Direction A's quiet desk with one honest team section. Needs you goes; the person's own work comes first; the cases become rows that say what they hold and who touched them last; and the one screen that can answer "what happened while I was away" does so from records the app already keeps, without a feed, a mail or a message. If the office is one person, B is A: the team section is never drawn.

Two things said plainly. First, "no needs you information" removes the amber signal that put a case under Needs you when retention was about to delete it; the retention warning stays on the Cases page's column, in the nightly digest mail and on the case page, so nothing is lost but the Home copy. Second, Direction B reads other people's notes' times and Gideon questions on shared cases; the app already shows both to everyone on the case, so no new privacy is opened, but it is new to see them gathered. Both are the maintainer's calls.
