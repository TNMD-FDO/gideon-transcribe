Bulk transcription: a place in the rail, and one flow made for it

A person who wants transcripts of a folder of files, and nothing else, today goes Upload files, three steps of settings, the batch page, then Home or My recordings for the download. The drawing adds one place to the rail under Home, Bulk transcription, whose page is that whole errand on one screen: drop the files, press Start, come back for one download. Nothing on Home moves; the cases, the incidents and the rest of the app are untouched. Made-up files.

Bulk transcription

Drop your recordings in, press Start, and come back for one download of every transcript. They are yours for this session and gone when you sign out; nothing goes into a case.

Drop recordings hereor choose filesUp to 50 files, 8 GB and 6 hours each. Most audio and video files work; not zip files.
Speakers, vocabulary, a case or a recording type: use Upload files instead.
StartLights when a file is in.

One place, one line. Bulk transcription sits under Home in the rail, on every page, lit while the person is on it, with its one line "Files in, transcripts out, nothing kept". The two actions at the top of the rail stay what they are: Upload files is still the full page with its settings and its cases.

The page opens on the drop. No "where do they go" (always this session only), no steps, no recording type. Two options only, the two a one-off most often needs: Translate to English and the spoken language, both as the Upload page asks them. Anything more sends the person to Upload files, in words on the page.

Under the hood it is a Batch. The same upload, the same line, the same transcription, the same download; the page is a shorter door to them, so nothing about the queue, the audit rows or the Admin's pages changes.

Three things only the maintainer can decide
  1. The name. "Bulk transcription" is the drawing's, from the ask; "Quick transcripts" or "Just transcribe" are the alternatives. The name goes into the glossary before it goes on a page.
  2. The two options. Translate to English and the spoken language are drawn; say if the page should ask nothing at all (detect the language, never translate) or one thing more, such as Separate speakers.
  3. Where in the rail. Under Home, as drawn, so a newcomer sees it second; or last among the places, under Clips, for an office where cases are the day's work and bulk transcription the exception.
