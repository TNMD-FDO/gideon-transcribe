Recommendation, and what the pick decides
  1. A if the 6 should be seen: a plain 6 anyone reads at 16 pixels, the voice inside it. D if the mark should mean transcription first: the quote that happens to be a 6, and the one solid mark that needs no tile. H if the 6 should be a quiet fact rather than a sign. B, C, E, F, G and I are drawn so the choice is made against them, not without them.
  2. The pick changes three things and nothing else: the mark in the top bar (`base.html`), the favicon (`favicon.svg`, drawn by hand as now, no licence), and the sign-in card's mark where no office logo is uploaded. An office logo an Admin has uploaded stays as it is.
  3. The two-weight name goes with every mark; say if the name should stay in one weight.
