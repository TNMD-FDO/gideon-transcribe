The Preservation copy: the first piece

The first of the plan's three releases: a whole case's work as one zip of PDFs, from the case's Details tab, any time. Deleting a case, the email and Archived come in the two releases after this and are not drawn here. Every name below is the local demo's made-up case.

As built (v1.244.0)
  • The documents in the copy are Word documents, not PDFs: where this drawing says PDF or ".pdf", read Word and ".docx". PDF was tried the day this was drawn and set aside; Phase 14 chapter 1 says why. Pick 4 (the kind of PDF) and pick 5 fell away with it.
  • Each recording has a folder of its own; pictures of the map and timelapses saved to the case go in; there is no count beside the button before a making starts; the copy is not listed on Home.
What this page holds
  • The picture of the Details tab as it stood is left out of this copy: no picture of a running app is kept in the repository.

The picks

One answer to each, please; my recommendation is marked.

PickThe choicesWhy I recommend it
1. Where it sits1a A part of its own above Delete case, Figure A recommended
1b A button beside Download all transcripts, Figure D		Room to say what it is for and to show it being made, and it is the last thing the eye passes before Delete case.
2. Who may make one2a The owner and everyone the case is shared with, as with every export today recommended
2b The owner alone		Whoever can already export each piece can already take the whole; a rule tighter than the exports' would only be worked round.
3. Is the made copy kept on the case3a Yes: the last one made stays, with when and by whom, until it is made again recommended
3b No: each press makes it and downloads it, and nothing is kept		A large case takes minutes, so the person should not have to wait at the page; and "made on" is itself a useful fact on the case. It counts against the owner's room like any file, but it is small.
4. The kind of PDF4a The archival kind (PDF/A), made for long keeping, with its typefaces inside the file recommended
4b Ordinary PDF		A file of record is opened years later, on machines we cannot foresee. The archival kind costs nothing extra to make.
5. PDF elsewhere in the app5a Only the Preservation copy for now; "as PDF" on the other export menus is a later, separate ask recommended
5b Add "as PDF" to every export menu in this release		The maker is the same, so 5b is cheap later; keeping it out now keeps this release to one thing.
Said so it is not a surprise: the typeface in the PDFs will be Carlito, a free face drawn to the same widths as Calibri, because Calibri itself cannot be put in the app; pages break in the same places and it looks very close, not identical. Download all transcripts stays as it is.
