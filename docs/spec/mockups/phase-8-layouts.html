4. The 49-inch screen: the incident page at full width and at half

A 49-inch ultrawide is 5120 by 1440 pixels; a window across half of it is 2560 by 1440, the same shape as an ordinary monitor. Today the incident page stops growing at about 1900 pixels, so at full width the cameras sit in the middle with margins either side.

  • The same rule for the other wide pages: the case page's two panes become three from 2560 (the list, the open recording's details or the search hits, the About pane); the recording page shows the transcript, the stage and the case list side by side from 2560.
