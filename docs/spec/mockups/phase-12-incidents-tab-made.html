The Incidents tab: after an incident is made

Three asks, the maintainer's words of 16 November 2026: "When an incident is made, the ticked cameras should auto clear"; "we need to make created incidents more prominent ... when incidents are created it's obvious where to find them"; and "Clear the ticks should be closer to where the ticks actually occur, so it's not confusing". Every name and time below is the local demo's made-up case.

What this page holds
  • The tab as it stands, on the local demo, with the three faults marked and one more found while looking.
  • Figure A: the tab with cameras ticked, as proposed. Figure B: the tab on coming back after Make the incident. Figure C: the other place the built incidents could sit. Figure D: a busy day.
  • Five numbered picks at the foot, each with a recommendation. Nothing is built until they are answered.

As it stands

The local demo at 1920 by 1080 with two cameras ticked.

The picture of the local demo with the faults marked is not kept in the repository; the list below is what it showed.

What is wrong
  • 1Clear the ticks is a screen away from the ticks. The tick boxes are at the far left of the lanes; the press that clears them is at the far right of the row above, about 1,500 pixels away at this width and more on an ultrawide.
  • 2The built incidents are in the least looked-at corner. They are a small pill in the top row ("6 incidents built") and a panel at the bottom right, under the map and under Your incident, with a small grey heading and most of its rows below the fold.
  • 3The ticks outlive the incident. Make the incident opens the new incident on its own page. The tab remembers the ticks, the span and the name for each day on this browser, so on coming back the same cameras are still ticked and the same name is still offered, as if nothing had been made.
  • 4Found while looking: Your incident is squeezed. With two cameras ticked the panel needs 258 pixels and gets 96 at 1080 high and 189 at 1440 high, because Built on this day takes the room below it. Its own Make the incident and Clear the ticks are scrolled out of view at every window size measured (1920, 2560 and 3440 wide).

The picks

Each has my recommendation marked. Answer by number, or "they stand" for all five as recommended.

PickThe questionThe choices
1What Make the incident does next.a. Opens the new incident on its own page, as today; the tab is clean and shows it as New on coming back (Figure B). recommended
b. Stays on the tab: the ticks clear, the new incident appears in the band as New with Open beside it, and the person can build the next one straight away.
2Where the built incidents sit.a. A band across the top of the tab (Figures A, B and D). It costs about 60 pixels of height from the lanes and the wall, and gives Your incident its room back. recommended
b. A panel at the top of the right column, above the map (Figure C). No height lost on the left; the map is shorter.
3How many Clear the ticks there are.a. One, at the head of the tick boxes (Figure A, C). The one in the top row and the one inside Your incident both go. recommended
b. The one at the head of the tick boxes, and Your incident keeps its own as well.
4A span the person set by hand (the Span box or a drag on the ruler), after the incident is made.a. It stays, since it has its own Clear the span and the person may be building several incidents inside it. recommended
b. It clears with the ticks, so the tab comes back wholly clean.
5Add the ticked (adding the ticked cameras to an incident already built).a. The ticks clear after it too, by the same rule, and that incident shows the green edge as "Added to". recommended
b. The ticks stay after Add the ticked; only Make the incident clears them.

Not changed by any of this: how an incident is made, what it holds, the incident's own page, the Map page, the wall, the map beside the strip, the lanes and the span. No setting and no change to the database: the ticks live on the person's own browser, so clearing them is done there.

As built (v1.243.0)

Where the build departs from the figures
  • The band and the lanes' head cost about 90 pixels of height together, not the 60 said under pick 2.
  • The New mark is kept for the browser tab: it goes when another incident is made or added to, or the browser tab is closed. Leaving the case cannot be told from the page.
  • The card of the one just made says "made by you" in place of its events.
  • Phase 12 chapter 10, "Once an incident is made, and where the built ones are", is the build.
