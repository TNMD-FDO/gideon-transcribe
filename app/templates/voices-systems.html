A jail call's announcements are the Call system's by their words (the phrase list on the Panel). Those lines teach the app the phone system's voice as each call lands; nobody keeps it from a card and it is never a person. A new phone system starts listening, not moving: the voice step counts the lines it would have given, and nothing on a transcript changes until an Admin starts the giving, having judged the numbers on the office's own calls. Given lines carry a pill and Not the system on the Speakers page; {{ back_to_stop }} lines given back in a month stop the giving by themselves.
